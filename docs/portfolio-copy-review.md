## Corrections and limits

- The rocket report gives 270.6 N·s total impulse and 0.2398 kg propellant mass, but also states 95.2 s specific impulse. These values do not agree under its stated gravity conversion. The unsupported specific-impulse figure is omitted pending a review of the original measurement and calculation; no replacement value is presented as a measured result.
- SAT and TOEFL participation entries were removed from honors/certifications. No test scores were introduced.
- Unconfirmed dates marked TODO for CIWS and rocket completion were removed. Ongoing Tern and AEROVIEW no longer carry fixed end dates.
- School documents are self-reported records, not independent verification. The site uses concrete task descriptions without converting reflections into measured impacts.
- Private source documents remain local. This review publishes their relevant factual scope, not raw records, other students' personal details, or internal company material.
- This is a copy and evidence review of existing entries, with the supported DAEMUN II omission added. It does not establish that every activity in the owner's history has been included.
